`<CascivoView>` renders 18 more components, the ones a page is built from: `PageHeader`, `Heading`, `Text`, `Stat`, `Status`, `Progress`, `Steps`, `Timeline`, `AvatarGroup`, `Image`, `Code`, `Blockquote`, `List` and `ListItem`, and the `CardHeader`, `CardTitle`, `CardContent` and `CardFooter` parts of a card. A JSON view can now carry a dashboard's header and KPI stats.
